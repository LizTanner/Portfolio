QA/Review Process Overhaul

I joined The Trevor Project's Training Team as one of two eLearning developers in June 2021 as two concurrent major curriculum refresh projects for the Lifeline and Digital Crisis Support teams were ongoing. Due to project delays resulting in a hard deadline for Lifeline, an external team managed the content QA, running directly behind the eLearning developers uploading SCORM modules for Lifeline's four-course training program to Litmos LMS. The QA was highly disorganized, assigning review criteria to 30+ individuals with their names and criteria only stated in a Google Sheet that wasn't communicated to reviewers, resulting in overlapping criteria and constantly changing reviewer assignments. This resulted in 1600+ rows of edits, many duplicated, that the eLearning developers implemented by making 800+ corrections each while simultaneously developing new Digital content. We were prohibited from sorting or filtering the sheet to identify duplicates resulting in a highly inefficient, four-week QA. Post-QA feedback and our Team's experience made it clear this was unsustainable and more efficient, streamlined QA strategy was needed.

QA/Review Process Flowchart

This four-phase workflow was designed to rectify the major issues our Training Team encountered including the lack of a clear workflow leading to continuous addition of edits, poor assignment design causing duplicate revisions, and failing to prioritize major content revisions prior to QA. The workflow sequences Internal Training Team tasks (blue) and External QA Team tasks (purple) per role in one process map. It delineates major and minor edits and enforces a cutoff of edit cycles by phase. Using Review 360 and shared CSVs in Phase I, stakeholders and leadership commented on major issues that Training revised prior to uploading to Litmos in Phase 2. This enabled a proper QA in Phase 3 focused on navigation errors, missing assets, and typos on the content in the LMS. The workflow also showed documentation export points (red outlined steps) to create receipt of all revisions being complete in an end-of-project "source of truth" Google Sheet.

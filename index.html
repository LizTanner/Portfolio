Welcome!

I am a Technical Training Consultant with over 20 years of extensive experience in the Learning & Development (L&D) and Systems Administration fields. My L&D expertise spans instructional design, eLearning development, learning management systems (LMS) administration, and project management roles in the K-12, higher ed, professional licensure/certification, and corporate user training (internal and external) industries. As a systems administrator primarily in Microsoft and Atlassian workspaces, I provide end-to-end solutions across platform integrations, app development, systems architecture, implementations, and operational strategies.

Using my combined L&D and systems administration experience, I consult SMBs and Enterprise organizations on solutions/systems architecture, platform workflow optimization, and end-to-end training development to delivery.

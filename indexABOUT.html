About Me

Career Profile

I started as a Science (6-12) instructional designer for a Curriculum Team at a virtual high school. When the small IT crew was bottlenecked by Fall enrollments, I took the initiative to learn basic HTML and edit Science-based content errors in IT tickets in the LMS. Quickly learning configurations and other LMS skills in both in-house and D2L LMSs, I earned Team dual roles as LMS admin and Science ID in six months. DeVry Inc. rewarded my efforts with the Professional Recognition of Integrity, Dedication, and Excellence (PRIDE) Award in 2012.

Next, I achieved several career firsts: my first consultant role for a private preK, my first WFH gig creating an SMB's database SOPs, and my first Storyline builds. The latter skilled became a career changer in my next ID-PM role for end-to-end development of real estate licensure/CE courses. As part of a content update initiative, I proposed replacing outdated content with Storyline modules. I demoed my builds for leadership, resulting in instant adoption.

My versatility earned my next role as the Trevor Project's first in-house eLearning Developer where I expanded my knowledge learning new app usage, API/integration development, and project management methodologies.

After going independent in 2023, I joined the Atlassian Community to grow on my system administration and app development skills. My active participation in the Atlassian Community earned me the Rising Star and the honor to host a live workshop building custom Jira and Confluence Forge apps with Atlassian clients.

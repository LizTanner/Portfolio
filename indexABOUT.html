About Me

Career Profile

I started as an instructional designer for grades 6-12 Science for the Curriculum Team at an online virtual school. When the small IT crew was bottlenecked by Fall enrollments, I took the initiative to learn basic HTML for resolving LMS content errors in IT tickets. I really enjoyed LMS work. Within 6 months, I could configure users/courses in both in-house and D2L LMSs, earning the dual role as Curriculum's LMS admin and Science ID. In 2012, DeVry Inc. recognized my efforts with the Professional Recognition of Integrity, Dedication, and Excellent (PRIDE) Award.

Next, I achieved several career firsts: my first LMS consultant role for a private preschool, my first WFH gig creating an SMB's database SOPs, and my first Storyline builds using Articulate 360 trials.

I also took a hybrid ID-PM role that managed the end-to-end development of licensure/CE courses for real estate professionals.
